# Saint Elms Fire

## Reimagining Learning and Knowledge Management in the Age of AI

Saint Elms Fire is the university of the future that gives every student their own personalised teacher with three minds. 

- *Socrates my Friend* is a buddy that knows yours logistics and deadlines

- *Socrates my Guide* knows the syllabus inside-out

- *Socrates my Philosopher* will challenge you to apply your knowledge to shape the world. 

Together, they teach from a living map of your personal learning journey. Every concept you touch becomes a star; the constellation of your knowledge grows with you over the duration of the course. 

Built to GDPR/DPDP standards, open-weight models do the work so the Elms brain runs on your local device. Your knowledge always stays under your control. It is yours to keep forever long after the end of the course.
